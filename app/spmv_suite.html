Selector accuracy (leave-one-out, 13 matrices)

  • GPU kernel (own): 13/13 with one rule, std nnz/row > 30 → vector-CSR else HYB (always-guess baseline 54%).
  • GPU incl. cuSPARSE: 62–85%, fragile. MPI partition: 31–46%, no better than guessing, so shown as low confidence.
  • OpenMP format and OpenMP-vs-MPI: 92% = majority baseline (one minority case, qpband).

What is integrated

  • T5 OpenMP: COO/CSR/ELL × schedules × threads, STREAM 12.8 GB/s.
  • T6 MPI: 1D-rows, 1D-nnz, 2D, 1–16 ranks, bytes sent per scheme.
  • T7 CUDA: scalar/vector CSR, ELL, HYB, cuSPARSE, with transfer times.
  • Live runs of the C/C++/CUDA code: use python suite.py on a machine with the toolchains.

Caveats

  • The three platforms ran on different machines; compare trends, not absolute levels.
  • n = 13 matrices; run-to-run noise ≈ 6%, so gaps under 5% are not meaningful.
  • Rules were fitted on these 13 matrices; custom-matrix platform timing is a rough estimate fitted to them.
